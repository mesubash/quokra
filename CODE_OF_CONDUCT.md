# Code of conduct

Quokra is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else
the project is discussed, is expected to:

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent. People here have different experience, languages and
  backgrounds.
- Keep it about the work: critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and explain why when they do.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexual language or imagery, publishing
someone's private information, and anything else that would make a reasonable person feel unwelcome.

**If something happens**, report it privately to the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile
([@mesubash](https://github.com/mesubash)), or through **Security → Report a vulnerability** if it's
urgent. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or block anyone
who breaks these rules.
