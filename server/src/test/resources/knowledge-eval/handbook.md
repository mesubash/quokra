# Orders

## Shipping
Orders placed before 2 pm on a working day leave our warehouse the same day. Standard delivery takes
2 to 4 working days; express delivery takes 1 working day and costs 9.90. Shipping is free for orders over 50.

## Tracking
You get an email with a tracking link as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse. The link works for
30 days.

## Changing an order
An order can be changed or cancelled until it leaves the warehouse. After that, refuse the parcel or
return it.

# Returns and refunds

## Returns
Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Print the return label from your account and
drop the parcel at any post office. Returns are free.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days after the return arrives.
Shipping costs are refunded only if the whole order is returned.

## Damaged items
If an item arrives damaged, send a photo within 7 days and we send a replacement at no cost.

# Account

## Password
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for one hour.

## Deleting your account
Write to support from the email address of the account. Accounts are deleted within 14 days, and
invoices are kept for 10 years as the law requires.

# Payments

## Payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards, bank transfer and invoice for business customers.

## Invoices
Invoices are emailed when the order ships and can be downloaded from your account.
